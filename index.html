Game concept / In development

Gilt & Guilt

Every fortune has a price.

A medieval alchemy and merchant adventure set on Avarra, a ring world where ambition, commerce, and a questionable sense of morality share the same market stall.

01 / The world

Avarra

Our direction is a stylized medieval world of settlements, trade routes, and discoveries beneath an unmistakable ring-world sky.

02 / The craft

Alchemy & trade

Gathering, brewing, and selling form the heart of the experience we're building. The enterprise grows as the game develops.

03 / The character

Deep, but funny

A world with history, eccentric inhabitants, and enough humor to keep all that ambition from taking itself too seriously.

These are design intentions, not a promise that every described feature is available in a public build. Website artwork is illustrative concept art, not gameplay.

Game project / Mining & progression

Pocket Vein.

There's fortune beneath your feet.

Our mining and machinery project explores the satisfaction of turning a modest operation into something more capable, one discovery and one upgrade at a time.

01 / Discover

Find the value

Dig into resources and the choices that make the next step of an operation worthwhile.

02 / Improve

Make it count

Visible equipment upgrades are an art and design goal. Improvement should show up in the machinery, not just the numbers.

03 / Keep going

One more upgrade

Stylized environments and expressive equipment bring character to a progression-focused game.

This overview includes our intended visual direction. The dedicated game website is the place to review its current state.

October 8 build.

Gilt & Guilt: Living development update list

This is a snapshot of the published studio devlog read for this website review. It reports progress as of October 8 at 11:40 PM Pacific. It does not automatically update, and the website review did not independently rerun the game tests.

Reported complete

  • 228 collectible prop groups, searchable crates and barrels, and improved tool attachment in third-person views.
  • Starter-route paving, planting, shop details, and softer daylight.
  • Isolated checks of the gather, brew, sell, and workshop-repair walkthrough.
  • Modern controls, key rebinding, hold/toggle choices, and a Classic preset.
  • Movement and animation fixes, plus isolated build, pickup, save/load, and alchemy checks.

Still in progress in this snapshot

Character appearance refinements, final movement and alchemy regression checks, manual native-input and Controls-menu review, and wider multiplayer testing.

Passing isolated tests does not establish launch readiness, certify performance, or replace broader playtesting.

Studio design notes / Intended direction

A little strange.
A lot of character.

Different games, a shared eye for personality.

01 / Cold Blooded

Cinematic hybrid

Obsidian surfaces, bone-ivory type, and controlled crimson accents. Three black creatures with red eyes anchor the studio identity.

02 / Gilt & Guilt

Crafted fantasy

Low-poly medieval environments, warm atmosphere, recognizable silhouettes, and a sense of scale under the ring-world sky.

03 / Pocket Vein

Expressive machinery

Faceted forms, distinct materials, atmospheric mines, and equipment intended to change visually as it improves.

The studio mascots use the approved Cold Blooded visual concept. Game-panel artwork is presentation art, not gameplay or a promise of either game’s final visual style.
